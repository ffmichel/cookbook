# Banana Ice Cream

**Yield:** 1 pint

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Frozen banana, very ripe | 230 g |
| Whole milk | 100 g |
| Heavy whipping cream | 100 g |
| Sweetened condensed milk | 80 g |
| Lemon juice | 5 g |
| Vanilla extract | 2 g |
| Salt | 0.5 g |
| Xanthan gum | 1 g |

### Method

1. Thaw the frozen bananas until they blend smoothly — frozen-solid chunks leave icy pockets.
2. Blend the banana with the milk, cream, condensed milk, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt until completely smooth.
3. Sprinkle in the xanthan gum with the blender running and blend another 15–20 seconds. Adding it all at once makes it clump.
4. Pour into a Ninja Creami pint, staying below the max fill line. Freeze 24 hours with the lid off.

### Spin Settings

Run **Ice Cream**. If crumbly, add a splash of cream to the center and **Respin**.

### Notes

- Two medium bananas is roughly 230 g. Weigh yours and scale the recipe to match.
- The riper and browner the bananas, the better — underripe banana tastes starchy once frozen.
- The lemon juice is there to keep the color from going gray, not for flavor.
- Mix-ins: chopped walnuts, or fudgy brownie chunks for a banana-brownie version.
