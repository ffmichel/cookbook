# Crème Pâtissière Sans Œufs

*Eggless pastry cream — no eggs in the filling, none in the crust wash.*

**Yield:** ~780 g, enough for one 26 cm (10-inch) tart | **Active time:** 20 minutes | **Chill:** 2 hours

Structure comes from the cornstarch, richness from the cream and butter. Firm enough to hold strawberries without slumping — closer to a classic pastry cream than to a butter-only version.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Whole milk | 480 g |
| Heavy cream (30% fat) | 120 g |
| Granulated sugar | 110 g |
| Cornstarch (Maïzena) | 54 g |
| Unsalted butter, cold | 54 g |
| Milk powder (optional) | 18 g |
| Fine salt | 1.5 g |
| Vanilla bean | 1 |

---

## Method

1. **Infuse (20 minutes).** Bring the milk, cream, and the split and scraped vanilla bean (pod included) just to a simmer. Cover, take off the heat, and let infuse.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, milk powder, and salt until no lumps remain. Remove the vanilla pod from the milk.
3. **Temper.** Pour a third of the hot milk over the dry mixture, whisking to a smooth paste, then pour it all back into the saucepan.
4. **Boil 2 minutes — the key step.** Over medium heat, whisking constantly. The thickening happens abruptly: keep it at a gentle boil for a full two minutes to cook out the starch.
5. **Finish with butter.** Off the heat, work in the cold butter a few cubes at a time until the cream is glossy.
6. **Chill (2 hours).** Spread onto a cold sheet pan, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface, and refrigerate until fully set.

---

## Notes

- **The two minutes of boiling are non-negotiable.** Without yolks, there is nothing to mask the taste of raw starch.
- **Spreading:** the cream sets firmer than an egg-based pastry cream. Whisk it vigorously to loosen, then spread it into the tart shell with a spatula rather than piping it.
- **Color:** without yolks the cream is ivory rather than yellow. A pinch of turmeric restores the usual tint under strawberries.
