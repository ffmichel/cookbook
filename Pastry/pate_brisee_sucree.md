# Pâte Brisée Sucrée

*Sweet shortcrust pastry — twice as much flour as butter, made by the breadcrumb method in a stand mixer.*

**Yield:** ~385 g, enough for one 23 cm (9-inch) pie or one 26 cm tart | **Active time:** 10 minutes | **Chill:** 30 minutes

A base recipe: other recipes link here for their crust. Lightly sweet, sturdy enough for wet fillings, and it rolls without cracking.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| All-purpose flour | 200 g |
| Cold unsalted butter, cubed | 100 g |
| Beaten egg | 50 g |
| Powdered sugar | 15 g |
| Ice water | 15 g |
| Lemon juice | 5 g |
| Fine salt | 1.5 g |

---

## Method

1. **Rub in the butter.** Put the flour, salt, and powdered sugar in the stand mixer bowl and add the cold butter. Mix with the flat beater on speed 2 until it looks like breadcrumbs.
2. **Bring it together.** Add the beaten egg, lemon juice, and ice water. Mix just until the dough clumps. If dry crumbs remain, add more ice water 5 g at a time, up to 15 g extra.
3. **Chill (30 minutes).** Turn out onto a lightly floured surface, knead two or three times, shape into a flat disc, wrap, and refrigerate.
4. **Roll and line.** Roll to about 3 mm thick, line the pan, and crimp or trim the edge. Chill again while the oven heats.

---

## Blind baking

Line the chilled shell with parchment and fill with pie weights or dried beans.

- **Par-bake, for fillings that bake in the crust (custards, pumpkin):** 10 minutes at 400°F (200°C) with the weights, then about 5 minutes without, until the base looks dry and set. Fill and continue with the pie recipe.
- **Full bake, for fillings that are not baked (pastry cream, fresh fruit):** 15 minutes at 400°F (200°C) with the weights, then 10 to 15 minutes without, until golden all over. Cool before filling.

---

## Notes

- **Weigh the egg.** One large egg is about 50 g once beaten. Stop at 50 g if yours is larger, so the egg stays at 25% of the flour weight. Scaling the table keeps that ratio.
- **No egg wash.** The rim browns well enough from the sugar and butter.
- **Water is the only variable.** Flour absorbency changes with brand and season; start low and add only what the dough needs to hold together.
- **Make ahead:** the wrapped disc keeps 2 days in the refrigerator or 1 month in the freezer. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator.
