# Pumpkin Pie

*Eggless custard filling set with cornstarch — brown sugar forward, with a little condensed milk for sweetness.*

**Yield:** one 23 cm (9-inch) pie, 8 slices | **Active time:** 15 minutes | **Bake:** 55 to 60 minutes at 350°F (175°C) | **Chill:** overnight

Sweeter than a classic pumpkin pie. Cornstarch does the work the eggs normally would, so the filling only firms up fully once it is cold: bake it the day before.

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| [Pâte brisée sucrée](../Pastry/pate_brisee_sucree.md), one batch | 385 g |
| Pumpkin purée (one 15 oz can, not pie filling) | 425 g |
| Evaporated milk | 180 g |
| Brown sugar | 135 g |
| Sweetened condensed milk | 120 g |
| Cornstarch | 30 g |
| Vanilla extract | 5 g |
| Ground cinnamon | 4 g |
| Fine salt | 3 g |
| Ground ginger | 1 g |
| Ground nutmeg | 0.5 g |
| Ground cloves | 0.3 g |

---

## Method

1. **Par-bake the shell.** Make the [pâte brisée sucrée](../Pastry/pate_brisee_sucree.md), line a 23 cm (9-inch) pie plate, and par-bake it as described there: 10 minutes at 400°F (200°C) with weights, then about 5 minutes without, until the base looks dry and set.
2. **Lower the oven to 350°F (175°C).** Move a rack to the lower third.
3. **Mix the dry ingredients.** In a large bowl, whisk the brown sugar, cornstarch, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cloves, and salt until no lumps of starch or sugar remain. The cornstarch goes in raw; it cooks in the oven.
4. **Make the custard.** Add the pumpkin, evaporated milk, condensed milk, and vanilla. Whisk until completely smooth, or blend for 30 seconds for the silkiest texture.
5. **Bake (55 to 60 minutes at 350°F / 175°C).** Pour into the shell and smooth the top. Bake until the edges are set and slightly puffed and the center still wobbles like gelatin. Check from 45 minutes, and shield the rim with foil if it browns too fast.
6. **Cool (2 hours).** Cool on a rack to room temperature. The filling still looks soft at this point.
7. **Chill (overnight).** Refrigerate uncovered at least 4 hours, ideally overnight. Do not slice early.

---

## Notes

- **Eggs:** the filling has none. The crust contains egg, 50 g to 200 g of flour, fully baked.
- **Taste before baking.** With no raw egg in the filling, you can taste it for spice and salt. It will taste a little starchy raw.
- **Small spice weights.** The nutmeg and cloves are below what most kitchen scales read: a generous pinch of nutmeg and a small pinch of cloves.
- **Pull it while it wobbles.** It looks underdone, but the starch finishes setting in the refrigerator. Pulled much earlier, it stays loose and tastes chalky.
- **Brown sugar:** dark brown gives a stronger molasses note.
